# 🧙 Mindtraveller

*A journey through code, AI, and endless possibilities.*

## Who Am I?

My name is **Augusto M.**, and I'm a software developer with many years of experience in the industry.

Throughout my career, I've witnessed technologies evolve, frameworks rise and fall, and development practices continuously change. But the arrival of AI brought something different.

For the first time in a long time, I started feeling that I was falling behind.

Instead of simply trying to keep up with the changes, I decided to turn this feeling into an opportunity: **build something meaningful, learn new technologies, experiment with modern development practices, and, most importantly, have fun along the way.**

That's how Mindtraveller was born.

## What Is This Project?

**Mindtraveller** is an AI-powered, text-based RPG where artificial intelligence creates and narrates adventures dynamically.

The idea is to create an interactive experience in which players explore worlds, encounter characters, make decisions, face challenges, and shape their own stories through text.

But there's more to this project than building a game.

Mindtraveller is also my personal laboratory: a place to experiment with AI-assisted development, explore new architectures, test technologies, and put modern software engineering concepts into practice.

The game is the destination, but the journey is just as important.

## What Can You Find Here?

This repository is intended to be the complete development journal of Mindtraveller.

I want to document not only the final implementation but also everything that happens along the way.

You'll find documentation covering:

- **Architecture & Design:** Architectural decisions, system design, technical specifications, and implementation strategies.
- **Development:** Features, experiments, code organization, and implementation details.
- **AI & LLMs:** AI integration, prompt engineering, context management, agentic workflows, and other AI-related concepts.
- **Studies & Research:** Technologies, frameworks, tools, and concepts I'm exploring.
- **Thoughts & Decisions:** Ideas, challenges, mistakes, lessons learned, and the reasoning behind important decisions.
- **Experiments:** Proofs of concept and technical explorations that may or may not become part of the final product.

Every folder in this repository represents part of this journey.

Some documents will describe finished features. Others will contain unfinished ideas, experiments, questions, or even approaches that didn't work.

And that's intentional.

## The Philosophy

I don't want this to be just another project built by following tutorials or copying existing solutions.

I want to understand the technologies I'm using, challenge my assumptions, explore different approaches, and document what I learn.

AI is changing the way software is built. Rather than watching that transformation from the sidelines, I want to be actively involved in it.

**Mindtraveller is my way of learning by building, experimenting by creating, and having fun while navigating the new era of software development.**
